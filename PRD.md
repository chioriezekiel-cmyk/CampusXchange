# Student Marketplace – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Introduction

The Student Marketplace is a website that will help students buy and sell items within their school. Students can use the website to advertise products they want to sell and also find items they need.

The main purpose of this project is to make buying and selling easier for students. Instead of depending only on WhatsApp groups or asking friends, students can visit one platform to see different products available for sale.

# Problem Statement

Many students find it difficult to buy and sell items within their school. Some students advertise their products on WhatsApp groups, but the messages can easily get lost when people send other messages.

Students who want to buy something may also find it difficult to know which students are selling that particular item. Some buyers are also worried about dealing with people they do not know.

Because of these problems, students need a simple platform where they can advertise products, search for items and contact sellers.

# Research Findings

To understand the problem better, we used sample research information from student interviews and surveys.

The sample research involved 5 students who were interviewed and 20 students who answered the survey. This gave us a total of 25 students.

The findings showed that:

- 12 students use WhatsApp groups to advertise their products.
- 5 students depend on friends and classmates.
- 3 students use Instagram or Facebook.
- 11 students said it is difficult to find products in WhatsApp groups.
- 6 students said they sometimes have problems trusting sellers.
- 5 students said sellers do not get enough attention.
- 3 students said some products remain advertised even after they have been sold.

Students also suggested some features they would like to see on the platform:

- 15 students wanted sellers to upload product pictures and prices.
- 13 students wanted a search feature.
- 12 students wanted a way to contact sellers.
- 10 students wanted seller verification.
- 8 students wanted a report button for suspicious users or products.


# Proposed Solution

Our proposed solution is to develop a Student Marketplace website where students can buy and sell items easily.

Students will be able to create accounts, upload products, add prices and pictures, and search for products they want to buy.

Buyers will be able to view product details and contact sellers directly. Administrators will also be able to manage the platform and deal with reported accounts or products.

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of the Student Marketplace are:

- To make buying and selling easier for students.
- To provide one platform where students can advertise their products.
- To help buyers find products without searching through many WhatsApp messages.
- To allow sellers to reach more students.
- To improve trust between buyers and sellers.
- To provide a simple and easy-to-use website.

# Target Users

The platform will have three main types of users.

* Students (Buyers)

Students who want to purchase products will be able to create accounts, search for products, view prices and contact sellers.

* Students (Sellers)

Students who want to sell products will be able to create accounts, upload product pictures, add descriptions and set prices.

* Administrator

The administrator will manage the website, check reported products and users, and remove inappropriate content when necessary.

# Main Features

# User Registration and Login

Students will be able to create an account using their basic information.

They will also be able to log in and log out of their accounts.

# Product Upload

Sellers will be able to upload products they want to sell.

They will provide:

- Product name.
- Product description.
- Product price.
- Product picture.
- Product category.

# Product Search

Buyers will be able to search for products by entering the product name or selecting a category.

# Product Details

Each product will have a page showing its picture, name, description, price and seller information.

# Contact Seller

Buyers will be able to contact sellers to ask questions or discuss how to purchase the product.

# Edit and Delete Products

Sellers will be able to edit their product information or remove products they no longer want to sell.

# Mark Product as Sold

Sellers will be able to mark products as sold so that buyers do not continue contacting them about unavailable items.

# Report Product or User

Students will be able to report suspicious products or users to the administrator.

# Admin Dashboard

The administrator will be able to manage users, view reports and remove inappropriate products.

# How the Website Will Work

A student first visits the Student Marketplace website.

If the student does not have an account, they will register. If they already have an account, they can log in.

After logging in, the student can choose to buy or sell products.

A student who wants to buy something can search for a product, view its details and contact the seller.

A student who wants to sell something can upload the product picture, enter the name, description and price, and publish it.

When the product is sold, the seller can mark it as sold or remove it from the website.

# Functional Requirements

The website should allow users to:

- Register and log in.
- View available products.
- Upload new products.
- Edit and delete their own products.
- Search for products.
- View product information.
- Contact sellers.
- Mark products as sold.
- Report suspicious users or products.

The administrator should be able to:

- View registered users.
- View uploaded products.
- Review reported products.
- Remove inappropriate products.
- Manage user accounts.

# Non-Functional Requirements

The website should be easy to use, even for students who are not very familiar with technology.

It should also:

- Load pages quickly.
- Protect users' account information.
- Work on mobile phones and computers.
- Store product information properly.
- Be available when students need it.
- Prevent unauthorized users from accessing admin features.

# Database Requirements

The system will need a database to store information about users and products.

The database will contain:

* Users

- User ID
- Full name
- Email address
- Password
- Role
- Date registered

* Products

- Product ID
- Product name
- Description
- Price
- Product image
- Category
- Seller ID
- Product status

*Reports

- Report ID
- User ID
- Product ID
- Reason for report
- Date reported

# Project Scope

The first version of the Student Marketplace will focus on the main features needed for buying and selling.

The project will include:

- Student registration and login.
- Product uploading.
- Product searching.
- Product details.
- Contacting sellers.
- Editing and deleting products.
- Marking products as sold.
- Basic admin management.

Features such as online payment, delivery tracking and mobile applications will not be included in the first version.

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

- Students can register and log in successfully.
- Sellers can upload products.
- Buyers can search for products.
- Buyers can contact sellers.
- Sellers can update product information.
- Sold products can be marked as sold.
- Administrators can manage reports.
- Students can use the website without much difficulty.

# Risks and Possible Solutions

* Fake sellers: Some people may create accounts to deceive buyers.

Solution: The website should have reporting features and administrator monitoring.

* Wrong product information: Some sellers may upload incorrect prices or descriptions.

Solution: Sellers should be responsible for providing correct information, and buyers should confirm details before purchasing.

* Poor internet connection: Some students may have difficulty using the website because of slow internet.

Solution: The website should be simple and should not use unnecessary heavy features.

* Inactive sellers: Some sellers may forget to update their products after selling them.

Solution: Sellers should have the option to mark products as sold or delete them.

# Security and Privacy

The website should protect students' personal information.

Passwords should be stored securely, and users should only be allowed to edit their own products.

Only administrators should have access to admin features.

Students should also be careful when meeting sellers or making payments. The platform will help students find one another, but it should not guarantee every transaction.

# Future Improvements

In the future, the Student Marketplace may include:

- Online payment options.
- Student verification.
- Product reviews and ratings.
- In-app messaging.
- Delivery arrangements.
- Notifications when new products are uploaded.
- A mobile application.

These features can be added after the first version of the website has been completed.

* Assumptions

The project assumes that students have access to smartphones, computers or internet connections.

It also assumes that students will provide correct information when creating accounts and uploading products.

The website will initially be used by students within the school community.

* Conclusion

The Student Marketplace will provide a simple way for students to buy and sell items within their school.

It will reduce the difficulty of searching for products through different WhatsApp groups and make it easier for sellers to advertise their products.

The project will start with basic features such as registration, product uploading, searching and contacting sellers. More features can be added in the future based on students' needs and feedback.
